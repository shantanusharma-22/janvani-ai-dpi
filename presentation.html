The Structural Crisis in Public Spending

Fragmented Citizen Signals & Misallocated Budgets

State and Central ministries allocate thousands of crores annually, yet acute infrastructure deficits persist across India's remote and tribal hamlets.

Departmental Data Silos

Grievances live in isolated portals (CPGRAMS, state call centers, paper applications). Roads, water, and health departments never coordinate capital works.

Capital Misalignment

Budgets are frequently disbursed via political lobbying rather than empirical citizen distress. Meanwhile, crores in District Mineral Foundation (DMF) funds sit unspent.

Zero Closed-Loop DPI

Citizens who raise infrastructure demands have no transparent tracking token or way to verify whether multi-crore public projects actually resolved their distress.

Root Cause: Absence of an automated semantic bridge between bottom-up vernacular demand and top-down GIS master planning.
