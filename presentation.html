Algorithmic Prioritization

The Public Value Index (PVI) Formula

An objective, transparent scoring metric that eliminates political bias and prioritizes high-ROI sovereign investments.

PVI = (0.30 × Demand) + (0.25 × Vulnerability) + (0.25 × Deficit) + (0.20 × Multiplier) - Friction

Citizen Demand (30%)

Normalized frequency, sentiment distress, and cross-village recurrence of complaints across Bhashini channels.

Vulnerability (25%)

NITI Aayog MPI index, SC/ST tribal concentration, infant/maternal mortality baseline from Census data.

Physical Deficit (25%)

Jal Jeevan Mission tap coverage, PMGSY unpaved kilometers, and distance to the nearest secondary hospital.

Multiplier (20%)

Economic spillover, agricultural market links, and alignment with PM Gati Shakti national logistics corridors.
