MULTI-DATA FUSION PIPELINE

How JanVani Synthesizes Disparate Datasets

Raw citizen feedback is noisy and fragmented. JanVani fuses bottom-up voice signals with three authoritative national datasets to eliminate bias, detect systemic neglect, and justify public investment.

Layer 1 // Bottom-Up Demand

Multilingual Citizen Signals

Aggregated from WhatsApp IVR, toll-free 1915 helplines, Gram Sabha resolutions, and CPGRAMS grievances. Geotagged to Local Government Directory (LGD) village codes with NLP urgency scoring.

  • Dialect Speech-to-Intent (ASR)
  • Sentiment & Recurrence Tracking
  • Cross-Validation with Neighbouring Villages
Layer 2 // Demographics

National Demographic Baseline

Correlated with 2011/2026 projected Census, Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC), Aspirational Districts indices, and SC/ST tribal concentration maps to ensure equitable capital distribution.

  • NITI Aayog Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI)
  • Vulnerable Demographics (Infant/Maternal Ratios)
  • Remote Tribal & Forest Hamlet Weighting
Layer 3 // Gap Analysis

National Infrastructure Deficit

Live APIs from Jal Jeevan Mission (tap water %), PMGSY (road connectivity %), National Health Systems Resource Centre (PHC beds/10,000), and BharatNet fiber connectivity.

  • JJM Tap Water Deficit Index
  • PMGSY Habitation Connectivity Gap
  • PHC / Sub-Center Emergency Radius Deficit
Layer 4 // Investment Alignment

Public Outlay & Gati Shakti Master Plan

Overlaid against Union Budget Capital Outlay (₹11.11 Lakh Cr), National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP), State Capital Budgets, and unspent District Mineral Foundation (DMF) funds.

  • PM Gati Shakti 44 GIS Data Layers
  • Overlapping Works Deduplication (No Repeated Digging)
  • District Unspent Fund Optimization

JanVani Public Value Index (PVI) Scoring Equation

PVI = (0.30 × Demand_Intensity) + (0.25 × Vulnerability_Index) + (0.25 × Infrastructure_Deficit) + (0.20 × Economic_Multiplier) - Friction_Penalty

Guarantees transparent, explainable project prioritization. No arbitrary political favoritism: every high-priority recommendation is mathematically justified by citizen distress, demographic equity, and physical service gaps.
